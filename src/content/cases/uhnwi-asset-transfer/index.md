## The situation

The client, a Ukrainian high-net worth individual and business owner, needed to restructure his business interests and transfer assets worth £180 million across jurisdictions.

The transfer had to be executed in a way that avoided triggering UK capital gains tax – a complex exercise given the scale of assets involved and the interplay between UK and international tax regimes.

## What we did

- Designed the restructuring plan across multiple jurisdictions
- Structured the asset transfer to ensure no UK capital gains exposure
- Coordinated the holding architecture to support the client’s ongoing business operations
- Managed the tax residence and reporting implications of the transfer
- Worked with the client’s other advisors to ensure seamless execution

## The outcome

**£180 million in assets transferred with zero UK capital gains tax**. The restructured business continues to operate across jurisdictions with a holding architecture designed for long-term efficiency.
