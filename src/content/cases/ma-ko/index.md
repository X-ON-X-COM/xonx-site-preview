## The situation

MA-KO is a Ukrainian IT company that had outgrown its original corporate structure. As the business expanded internationally, the existing setup created tax inefficiencies and operational friction across jurisdictions.

The company needed a new corporate and operational architecture that could support international growth while ensuring tax efficiency on retained profits.

## What we did

- Analysed the existing corporate structure and identified inefficiencies
- Designed a new international holding and operational structure
- Structured the business to achieve 0% tax on retained profit
- Implemented the restructuring across multiple jurisdictions
- Ensured compliance with local regulatory requirements in each jurisdiction

## The outcome

A fully restructured corporate and operational framework that supports MA-KO’s international operations with **0% tax on retained profit** – freeing capital for reinvestment and growth.
