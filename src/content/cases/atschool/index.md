## The situation

AtSchool is a Ukrainian distance educational institution with over a decade of experience delivering remote education. With a proven model at home, AtSchool decided to launch in the UK market – bringing its distance learning platform to British students and families.

The company needed a corporate and operational structure that would satisfy UK regulatory requirements for educational providers while maintaining tax efficiency across both jurisdictions.

## What we did

- Designed the corporate structure for UK market entry
- Advised on regulatory requirements for distance education providers in the UK
- Structured the operational framework for cross-border educational service delivery
- Planned the tax architecture to support operations in both Ukraine and the UK
- Set up the UK entity and ensured compliance with local requirements

## The outcome

AtSchool **launched in the UK market** with a compliant corporate structure, a clear regulatory framework, and a tax-efficient operational model – extending over 10 years of distance education expertise to a new market.
