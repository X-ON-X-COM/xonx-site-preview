## The situation

Poynto is a new hotel concept – a travel social hub designed for modern travellers who value connection over luxury. It is founded by Vasily Grogol, prominent Ukrainian hotelier and entrepreneur.

The company needed a corporate and tax structure for its very first location, and support through the investment process – from structuring the holding architecture to coordinating with local providers and communicating with investors.

## What we did

- Designed the legal and tax structure for the first Poynto location
- Built the holding architecture to support the investment round and future expansion
- Supported Poynto through the investment process, including investor communications
- Coordinated with local providers across jurisdictions

## The outcome

**A launch-ready legal and tax structure** for Poynto’s first location – to be announced soon – with the holding architecture in place to support the investment round and scale to future locations.
