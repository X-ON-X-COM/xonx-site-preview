## The situation

Bazhane, one of Ukraine’s most iconic and viral fashion brands, approached us with a clear goal in mind - launch online sales across the EU, and later open a flagship boutique in Europe.

## What we did

- Built a supply chain, including all logistics from Ukraine to the distribution warehouse in Poland
- Structured export/import regulations
- Built legal framework for online store operations: opened legal entity in EU and the bank account
- Advised on corporate and indirect taxation
- Addressed compliance of the EU company
- Covered all legal aspects of stock management
- Addressed statutory bookkeeping requirements
- Structured the movement of goods and cashflow for EU operations together with Bazhane’s team

## The outcome

A tax-efficient setup enabling seamless **access to all 28 EU markets**.
